Risks

Coil heat: coils sit beside combustion; simulated temperatures are estimates. Mitigation: direct dielectric ester cooling, ceramic crown barrier. Test: single-cylinder thermal soak with embedded sensors.

Insulation: heat cycling and contamination can degrade winding insulation. Mitigation: Class-220 windings, sealed dielectric coolant. Test: insulation-resistance and high-potential tests after thermal cycling.

Vibration: laminated teeth on a reciprocating piston at high RPM. Mitigation: dovetail anchoring with compliant clips. Test: shaker and endurance runs, then teardown inspection.

Air gap: the cylinder liner wall counts as part of the magnetic gap, and force falls steeply with gap. Mitigation: tight tolerances, thin non-magnetic liner design. Test: FEM with the real liner thickness, then bench measurement.
